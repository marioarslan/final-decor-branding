Who we are

A residential design studio in Aleppo, building homes that feel calm, warm and exact.

Final Decor was founded on a simple frustration: too many beautiful drawings that fell apart on site. So we built a studio that does both — design and construction under one roof, one team, one standard.

Over a decade and more than a hundred and twenty homes later, our work is known for warm modern interiors: honest materials, layered light, and a quiet precision you feel more than you notice.
